About Frequency Lab

Frequency Lab is a local-first interactive platform for exploring auditory frequencies used in research and spiritual traditions.

Design Principles

  • No backend required — runs entirely in the browser.
  • All frequency data is stored locally in JSON.
  • Real-time sound generation via Web Audio API (no audio files).
  • Scientific claims are clearly separated from traditional/spiritual claims.
  • No frequency is presented as proven medical treatment.

Audio Safety
